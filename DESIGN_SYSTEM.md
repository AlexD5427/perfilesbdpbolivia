# Liquid Glass HR design system

The visual language uses calm banking navy, productive teal and warm gold accents over translucent surfaces. Glass is decorative, never a substitute for contrast. Fallbacks remain readable without backdrop blur. Responsive layouts reduce transparency and shadow density on small screens.

Motion is limited to transform and opacity, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and never carries essential information. Future media components must provide stable dimensions, alt text, captions, transcripts and reduced-motion poster fallbacks.
